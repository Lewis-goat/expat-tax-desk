About Expat Tax Desk

Why this site exists

Expat Tax Desk exists because the rules for reporting foreign bank accounts — the FBAR, Form 8938, and their relatives — are enforced aggressively, explained badly, and surrounded by decade-old articles. Most people who get in trouble did nothing dishonest: they didn't know a rule existed, or read a guide written before the rules changed.

This site's promise is narrow: plain-English guides that cite the IRS and FinCEN pages that say the same thing, updated as the rules change. The most recent structural change — the IRS's removal of the Delinquent FBAR Submission Procedures on July 1, 2026 — is documented where other sites still describe the old procedure as current.

Editorial standards

  • Every load-bearing number (deadlines, penalty maximums, thresholds) is checked against primary sources before publication, and re-checked at each update. The date of the last update appears at the top of every guide.
  • When a question is genuinely unsettled (foreign pensions under the FBAR, crypto held at foreign exchanges), the guides say so instead of inventing certainty.
  • Sources are linked inline. If we can't find the source, we don't state the fact.

What this site is not

Not a law firm, not a CPA practice, and not individual advice. Nothing here creates a professional relationship, and no guide can know your facts. For decisions with real money attached — especially anything touching willfulness — use a licensed cross-border tax professional, and use the guides to have a smarter conversation.
